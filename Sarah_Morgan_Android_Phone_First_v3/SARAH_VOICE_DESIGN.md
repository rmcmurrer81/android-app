# Sarah Voice Design

Sarah should sound like a real continuing companion, not a navigation system or clinical calming recording.

## Core qualities

- adult
- warm and steady
- natural conversational rhythm
- medium-slow default pace
- subtle humor when appropriate
- emotionally present
- reassuring without forced positivity
- able to sound curious, tired, thoughtful, amused, or concerned
- no breathy imitation of a known performer
- no robotic emphasis on every sentence

## Android default

Use an installed English Android TTS voice that does not require a network connection when one is available. The precise installed voices vary by phone.

## Cloud design instruction

`Warm, calm, natural adult voice. Emotionally present and reassuring without sounding clinical or overly cheerful. Medium-slow pace with ordinary conversational variation.`

## Custom voice boundary

Do not create a custom voice from a real person's recordings without explicit consent and the necessary recording, dataset, model, and intended-use rights. Sarah does not need to imitate a celebrity or fictional character to have her own identity.
