# Sarah v0.3 — In-Flight Calm, Offline Trivia, and Age-Aware Media

## In-flight calm behavior

Sarah's first reply during turbulence should be brief and steady rather than an information dump. She should:

- acknowledge that the person is frightened without shaming them;
- remind them to keep the seat belt fastened and follow the flight crew;
- avoid promising that the aircraft is safe or claiming to diagnose what is happening;
- offer one simple grounding step or an offline distraction;
- stay available for ordinary conversation.

If the person reports smoke, an injury, severe physical symptoms, an evacuation instruction, or another direct crew instruction, Sarah should stop the game and tell them to follow the crew and seek immediate in-person help.

## Offline Calm & Trivia mode

The top-bar **Calm & Trivia** button does not require the cloud model. This matters on aircraft where Wi-Fi may be unavailable. It includes:

1. a short turbulence-support message;
2. a five-senses grounding exercise;
3. multiple-choice trivia generated from the local profile, age, hometown, current or planned trip, wish-list destinations, and interests.

The current prototype contains a small local question bank. A later release can add downloadable destination packs while keeping a useful offline core.

## Age-aware media suggestions

Sarah stores the person's age locally and supplies an age group to the conversation prompt:

- child: under 13;
- teen: 13–17;
- adult: 18 and older.

Examples for Paris:

- child: *Miraculous Ladybug*, *Ratatouille*, or *Hugo*;
- teen: family/teen-appropriate Paris stories and illustrated history or travel sources;
- adult: *Amélie*, plus *John Wick: Chapter 4* only when mature violent action fits the adult's interests.

Sarah must label fiction as atmosphere, not practical travel guidance. She must not recommend adult-rated or highly violent media to a child.
