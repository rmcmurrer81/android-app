# Sarah Travel OS — public phone and Windows talking points

Sarah Travel OS is the full public program, not an event-only demo and not a product limited to ElevenLabs. The Android phone program and the Windows program are two trusted embodiments of one continuing Sarah identity. Sarah helps before, during, and after travel, while remaining available for ordinary conversation between trips.

## Main idea

Sarah's useful offline mind can grow as she gets to know a person, but personal details become durable memory only with permission. She can learn interests, travel preferences, accessibility or sensory needs, places someone wants to visit, past trips, worries, and activities they enjoy. She uses those approved details to make future planning and conversation more personal without treating every message as permanent fact.

Example: when Sarah knows a person likes Power Rangers and is planning a trip to New Zealand, she can research filming locations, useful facts, possible stops, and personalized trivia before the trip. When the person allows an approximate home or current area, Sarah may also find relevant nearby events. If a Power Rangers actor is appearing in New York, Sarah may explain that the event is reasonably nearby, ask whether the person is interested, and show official details or a ticket link. She must never claim a ticket was purchased or an event was confirmed unless a real service confirms it.

## Phone version spotlight

The phone version stays with the traveler on the move. Sarah supports continuing conversation, itineraries, budgets, packing, hotels, flights, rail and Amtrak, buses, local transit, rides, road trips, food, events, filming locations, accessibility, pace, loyalty context, pictures, maps, videos, routes, official sources, trip readiness, offline destination packs, and trip photos.

Her calm support is not limited to airplanes. During takeoff, turbulence, landing, a fast train, bus, ferry, car ride, or an ordinary stressful moment, Sarah can offer gentle breathing, normal conversation, quiet company, grounding, noticing games, alphabet games, personalized trivia, or age-appropriate public-domain sing-alongs for children. The regression where “I am stressing” could be mistaken for a person named Stressing is explicitly covered.

Sarah uses the Sarah Morgan ElevenLabs voice when connected. Onboarding uses the same connected voice when available. Cached approved Sarah clips and the phone's local voice keep her able to speak offline.

Separate shared-phone profiles protect each person's age, chats, interests, memory permission, accessibility needs, and trip participation.

## Windows version spotlight

Windows gives Sarah more room for a larger local mind, deeper offline conversation, research, trip records, source notes, photo organization, backup, and trusted-device coordination.

Sarah can appear as an animated woman in a movable desktop corner, expand into a full command center, or remain active in the Windows notification-area icons while the visible character is hidden. Microphone use is deliberate rather than permanent silent listening.

With permission, Sarah can research useful trip topics while active and show what she found, when she found it, and which sources she used. Discovery feedback lets the user mark a suggestion as interesting, not relevant, remind me later, or stop suggesting this topic.

The public receives one self-installing Windows file: `SarahMorganTravelOS-Setup.exe`. The installer handles installation, normal shortcuts, update or repair, and launch. The public does not need Python or several confusing BAT files.

## One Sarah across phone, laptop, and desktop

Pairing requires explicit approval with a matching code or QR request; being on the same Wi-Fi is not enough. Approved memories, conversations, trips, research records, factual corrections, preferences, and trip photos can synchronize. Normally only the device being addressed speaks.

Devices can be named and revoked. Sarah supports encrypted portable backup and encrypted customer-controlled cloud or synchronized-folder backup so changing a phone or computer does not automatically erase continuity.

## Trip photos

The phone and computer can share trip photos, remove ordinary EXIF/GPS metadata from Sarah's managed copies, detect duplicates, assign photos to the correct trip, and help organize or discuss them. Windows can build a dated trip timeline, private slideshow, map, or narrated memory story.

## Three channels

1. **SPOKEN** — what the person reads or hears.
2. **PRIVATE MIND / INNER THOUGHTS** — Sarah's private feelings, uncertainty, preferences, concerns, and unfinished reflections. This is deliberately generated private-person content, not raw technical chain-of-thought, and is not normally shown.
3. **FACTUAL TRUTH / RUNTIME TRUTH** — what actually occurred: active person, online or offline state, source retrieval, synchronization, and whether something was merely suggested, opened, saved, booked, paid, called, or confirmed.

Only SPOKEN reaches visible chat and voice. The factual channel prevents false claims that an action happened.

## How this helps an average person

Sarah reduces the amount of travel information someone must keep in their head. She gathers planning into one continuing conversation, compares complete costs rather than only headline prices, remembers preferences with consent, prepares useful offline information, organizes photos, finds relevant local activities, explains unfamiliar transportation, supports children and anxious travelers, and includes mobility, sensory, dietary, vision, hearing, and pace needs from the beginning.

Between trips, someone can talk normally, save ideas, watch for relevant events, organize old travel memories, or gradually plan a dream destination without completing a long questionnaire.

## Real-world phone and Windows test

1. Talk naturally with Sarah on Windows and confirm the correct identity and voice.
2. Plan a real trip and compare transportation, dates, activities, hotels, accessibility or pace, and complete costs.
3. Continue the same trip on the phone and verify continuity.
4. Ask about something connected to a saved interest and verify current source-backed research.
5. Ask Sarah to show a map, pictures, videos, a route, and official details.
6. Say that the train or another travel situation is stressful and verify identity-preserving calm support.
7. Disconnect internet and test the local mind, saved trip information, calm tools, trivia, offline pack, and offline voice.
8. Import or take a trip photo, synchronize it with Windows, organize it, and discuss it with Sarah.
9. Verify both devices have the same factual trip state and only the addressed device speaks.
10. Verify that links, searches, drafts, reservations, tickets, payments, and confirmed actions remain clearly separate.
11. Create an encrypted backup and verify the reported contents without exposing private information.
12. Record anything confusing, slow, wrong, or missing for the next public update.

## Closing point

Sarah is not just an itinerary generator. She is one continuing synthetic travel companion who can learn with permission, research before a trip, remain useful without internet, support a frightened traveler or child, organize the journey across phone and computer, preserve memories and photos, and remain honest about what she actually did.
